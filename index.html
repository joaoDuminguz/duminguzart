Hello!

I’m João Duminguz, a game artist: 2D, pixel art and stylized 3D.

Fourteen years making things look like something: 2D and pixel game art, stylized 3D characters and props, concept art and the visual systems that keep a team consistent. I led the 3D art team at Sortium on a stylized cyberpunk Roblox title, and before that art directed independent games, a feature film and comics.

My own aesthetic, Parahyba Surrealism, puts the culture of northeastern Brazil next to science fiction. I work remotely, from João Pessoa, with studios anywhere.
