Colour

Ten brandbook values. Dark-first: ink is the ground, bone is the type, the other eight are accents and never surfaces.

Affordance · blue

“You can act on this.” Visible at rest on everything actionable, so it is the one genuinely reserved colour: blue on something inert is a promise the page does not keep.

Accent · clay

“Attention here.” Not reserved — hover and a marked term mean the same thing, so the uses reinforce rather than collide. Hover is one application, not the definition.

Response · orchid

“Your action registered.” On screen for about 100ms during a click. Nothing is learned in 100ms, so it is the freest colour in the system.
